Introduction to Smart Farming

Smart farming involves the management of farms using modern Information and Communication Technologies (ICT) to increase the quantity and quality of agricultural products.

Types of Crops

  • Food Crops: Wheat, Rice, Maize, Pulses
  • Cash Crops: Sugarcane, Cotton, Jute, Tobacco
  • Plantation Crops: Tea, Coffee, Rubber
  • Horticulture Crops: Fruits and Vegetables

Farming Methods

  • Traditional Farming
  • Organic Farming
  • Precision Farming
  • Hydroponics & Aeroponics

Modern Agriculture Technologies

  • IoT Sensors (Soil & Climate Monitoring)
  • Agricultural Drones for Crop Spraying
  • Automated Irrigation Systems
  • AI & Big Data Analytics for Yield Prediction

Farmer Tips

  • Perform regular soil testing to understand nutrient needs.
  • Adopt drip irrigation to save water and energy.
  • Practice crop rotation to maintain soil fertility and reduce pests.
  • Stay updated with weather forecasts to plan sowing and harvesting effectively.
